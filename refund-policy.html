Returns & Refund Policy

Transparent policies for a worry-free experience

At Prestige Window, we take pride in our precision-engineered products. Since most of our windows and doors are customized to your specific architectural measurements, our refund policy is designed to reflect the bespoke nature of our work.

1. Order Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled within 24 hours of the advance payment. Once the material procurement or manufacturing process starts based on your site measurements, cancellations are not permitted.

2. Customized Products

Please note that custom-made products (uPVC Windows, Doors, and Sliding Systems) are non-refundable and non-returnable once manufactured correctly according to the signed measurement sheet.

Note: As mentioned in our catalog, actual amounts vary as per requirements. Site visiting charges (₹100 - ₹500 as applicable) are non-refundable but will be adjusted in the final invoice upon order placement.

3. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a product that is damaged during transit or has a manufacturing defect:

  • Inform our installation team immediately at the time of delivery.
  • Send photos of the defect to prestigewindowalu@gmail.com within 48 hours.
  • We will replace the defective part or the entire unit at no additional cost after verification.

4. Refund Process

In rare cases where a refund is approved (e.g., overpayment or service unavailability):

  • Refunds will be processed to the original payment method.
  • It may take 7-10 working days for the amount to reflect in your account.

5. Installation Issues

If there is an issue with the installation, our "After-Sales Support" team will visit your site to rectify it. Refunds are not provided for minor installation adjustments.
